Questions

What happens to my data?

The Google Sheet and the Telegram bots belong to you, and you can withdraw my access at any time. I keep only what the work needs: for bookings, the customer's name, phone, service and time; for the inbox, the sorting results, not your email texts. The AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) receives only what each task needs, under its API terms. I sign a data processing agreement with you and share the provider's current terms with it. A clinic's bot keeps only the name, phone, service and time, with no medical details.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It can, so nothing important depends on the AI alone. Email drafts are never sent on their own, and a draft that mentions a figure missing from your facts starts with a warning. A phone number or budget is saved only if it is really in the email. The bot books only when the customer presses "Confirm". A competitor price read by AI is accepted only if its quote is found on the page. Emails that try to give orders to the AI are flagged, and nothing is done with them.

Do I need to install new software?

No. You keep your mailbox, Telegram and Google Sheets. Your customers book in Telegram, with no extra app. The system runs on my server as part of the support plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are monthly, and you can cancel any time. The service runs to the end of the paid month, then stops. The Google Sheet and the Telegram bots stay yours. On request, I export your bookings or price history as CSV files. Then I delete your data from my server within 30 days.

What else will I pay for?

Only other services you choose to use, billed by them. There are no charges for AI use above your plan: its AI operations are a monthly cap. If you reach it, I tell you, and until the 1st, or until you move to a higher plan, the AI pauses: the bot falls back to simple rules, new emails wait to be sorted, and pages without price markup are not checked.

How do I pay?

For setup, 50% before I start and 50% after you accept the work. Support is invoiced on the 1st of each month, in advance. If you found me on Upwork or Fiverr, every payment goes through that platform.

Do you guarantee results?

I don't promise more revenue, a set number of bookings or a set drop in no-shows: these depend on your customers. I promise that the system works as described here, and if it doesn't, I fix it.
